A homepage is an editorial decision, not a feature inventory. It decides the size of the first sentence, the distance to the first image, and whether a reader should scroll or pause.

## Decide the reading speed

![A homepage rhythm study made from grids, light, and paper planes](/images/articles/a-homepage-has-a-tone/detail.svg)

A good homepage sets a rhythm: expansive at the opening, denser in the middle, spacious again at the end. Content begins to feel like an essay rather than a stack of modules.

## Let constraints become voice

Voice comes from repeated constraints. Consistent alignment, a stable type scale, and restrained color quietly tell readers what this place values.
