Technology products often want to appear limitless. For users, knowing clearly what a system cannot do can be more valuable than another list of capabilities.

## Boundaries create expectations

Boundaries create accurate expectations and force a team to define what the product truly owns. Ambiguous capability eventually becomes ambiguous failure.

## Make the process visible

When software admits uncertainty, shows its process, and allows undo, it stops being a mysterious machine and becomes a tool we can work alongside.
