Feeds reward immediate reaction. Writing asks a vague feeling to become a sentence that can be examined. They do not run on the same clock.

## Writing needs another clock

Slow writing need not be long. It simply lets an idea survive one more round of doubt before publication, without shaping every sentence into a hook.

## Readers can feel time

Readers can feel that time. It lives in the rhythm of a paragraph, an uninflated headline, and the places where a writer is willing to leave space.
