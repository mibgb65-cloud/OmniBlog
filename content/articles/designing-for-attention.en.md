Every interface arranges attention. Badges, counters, and autoplay are never neutral; they train us to expect the next change.

## Attention is not infinite

Restraint is not dullness. It reserves surprise for moments that matter and returns to readers the right to decide when they are done.

## Begin with a quieter default

Designers cannot manage a user’s life, but they can begin with a quieter default: no flashing, no pressure, and no assumption that time-on-site is the only answer.
